Bubble wrap was invented to go on your walls! As wallpaper. And nobody wanted it.

In nineteen fifty-seven, two engineers in New Jersey sealed two plastic shower curtains together. Air got trapped inside, making hundreds of little bubbles.

They thought this was the future of home design. Bumpy, textured wallpaper!

But nobody wanted bubbles on their walls. It was a total flop.

So they tried again. Greenhouse insulation! Bubbles to keep the plants warm.

That flopped too.

Then IBM built a huge new computer. And they needed a way to ship it without breaking it.

So the engineers wrapped it in their bubbles. And it arrived perfectly safe!

Suddenly, every company wanted it. Their failed wallpaper became a billion-dollar business.

But here's the twist. Today, the company also sells a bubble wrap you can't even pop!

So next time you pop one... remember, you're popping a piece of failed wallpaper.
