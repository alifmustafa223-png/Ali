Chewing gum exists because of a dictator. And his failed plan to take back Mexico!

In Denmark, scientists found a lump of chewed birch tar, five thousand seven hundred years old. The chewer's DNA was still in it!

The ancient Greeks chewed tree resin to freshen their breath.

Then, in eighteen sixty-nine, Mexico's exiled general Santa Anna was living in New York.

He gave inventor Thomas Adams a tree sap called chicle. Turn it into rubber, he said, and we'll both get rich. Then I'll buy an army!

Adams tried for a year. Toys, boots, tires. Every test failed.

Then he saw a girl buying gum in a drugstore. So he boiled the chicle, rolled it into gum, and people loved it!

Santa Anna never got his army. He died poor in Mexico.

Years later, a soap salesman named William Wrigley gave away free gum. Customers wanted the gum more than his products!

So he sold gum instead, and built an empire.

Today, gum is so messy, Singapore banned selling it!
