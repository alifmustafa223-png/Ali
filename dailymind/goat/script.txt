Messi's first contract was written on a napkin! And Ronaldo needed heart surgery at fifteen.

Both of them almost never became footballers.

At eleven, Messi stopped growing. Doctors found a hormone problem, and the treatment cost about a thousand dollars a month.

His family couldn't afford it. So every night, little Leo gave himself an injection in his legs.

Then Barcelona came. At a restaurant, a club director grabbed a paper napkin and wrote the deal on it.

Years later, that napkin sold for almost a million dollars!

Meanwhile in Portugal, a skinny kid cried every time his teammates didn't pass. They called him Cry Baby.

At fifteen, doctors found his heart was racing way too fast. He had laser surgery... and was back training days later.

Fun fact: his dad named him after Ronald Reagan, his favorite actor.

Two kids who almost didn't make it, became the two greatest players of their time.
