What if the internet shut down for one whole day? By lunch, you couldn't even buy bread. Minute one. No messages, no maps, no videos. Annoying... but fine. Hour one. Card payments fail. Shops can only take cash, and almost nobody carries any. Hour three. Flights stop. Airlines need the internet to check in passengers and plan every route. Hospitals switch to paper. Trucks never get their orders, so shelves can't be restocked. This isn't fiction. In 2024, one bad software update crashed eight and a half million computers, and thousands of flights were cancelled. And here's the twist. In 2021, Facebook went offline for six hours, and some of its engineers couldn't even get into the building. Their key cards ran on the same network.
