Monopoly was invented to show that monopolies are evil! And the woman who created it was robbed.

In nineteen-oh-four, Lizzie Magie got a patent for a board game called the Landlord's Game.

She wanted to warn people that greedy landlords get richer and richer, while everyone else goes broke.

So her game had two sets of rules. In one, everybody shares the wealth. In the other, one player crushes everyone.

Guess which one people loved?

Players started making their own homemade boards. One version used street names from Atlantic City.

Then, in the nineteen thirties, a man named Charles Darrow copied it and sold it as his own invention.

The game became a giant hit, and Darrow became a millionaire.

And Lizzie? The company paid her just five hundred dollars for her patent.

For decades, the world believed Darrow invented it.

So the next time you play Monopoly... remember, it was supposed to be a warning.
