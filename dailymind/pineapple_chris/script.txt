In the seventeen hundreds, people rented pineapples, just to show off. And one man built a whole house shaped like one!

Because a single pineapple cost about eight thousand dollars in today's money.

In fourteen ninety-three, Columbus found this strange fruit in the Caribbean.

But on the trip home, almost every one rotted.

Europe tried to grow its own, but for two hundred years, the cold killed every plant.

Then gardeners found a disgusting trick. They heated their greenhouses with piles of rotting manure!

When a gardener finally handed one to King Charles the Second, it was a royal sensation.

Nobody ate them. They sat on tables like trophies, until they went bad.

So shops rented them out for the night. The same pineapple went to party after party.

A Scottish earl loved them so much, he built a house with a giant stone pineapple on top. It's still standing today!

Today, fast ships made them cheap. You can buy one for two dollars.
