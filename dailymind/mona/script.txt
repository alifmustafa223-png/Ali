The Mona Lisa wasn't famous... until someone STOLE it! Back then, most people had never even heard of her.

In 1911, Vincenzo Peruggia, a man who once worked at the Louvre, hid inside a closet overnight.

The next morning, he lifted the painting off the wall, hid it under his white work coat... and simply walked out.

Nobody noticed for over a day! The guards thought it was being photographed.

The Louvre closed for a whole week. And when it reopened, thousands lined up... to stare at an empty wall!

Police even questioned a young painter named Pablo Picasso!
