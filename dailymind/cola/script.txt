Coca-Cola was invented as medicine! And yes, it really had cocaine in it.

In eighteen eighty-six, a pharmacist named John Pemberton was in constant pain. A war wound had left him addicted to morphine.

He wanted a cure. So he mixed coca leaves and kola nuts into a sweet brown syrup.

Coca, from the leaves. Cola, from the nuts. That's where the name comes from!

He sold it as a brain tonic. It promised to cure headaches, tiredness, and even morphine addiction.

A glass cost five cents at a drugstore soda fountain. But in the whole first year, it sold only about nine glasses a day.

Pemberton never got rich. He sold off his rights, and two years later, he died almost broke.

Then a businessman named Asa Candler bought the formula for about two thousand three hundred dollars.

By the early nineteen hundreds, the cocaine was quietly taken out.

Today, the company sells more than two billion drinks every single day.
