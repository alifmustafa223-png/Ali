Purple was once worth more than gold! And it was made from snail slime.

Three thousand years ago, in the city of Tyre, dyers cracked open sea snails to get a tiny drop of purple.

It took about ten thousand snails to make just one gram of dye. And the workshops stank of rotting snails.

So purple became the color of kings. In Rome, purple silk was worth more than its weight in gold.

Some emperors even banned everyone else from wearing it.

Then, in eighteen fifty-six, an eighteen-year-old student named William Perkin tried to make a medicine for malaria.

His experiment failed. All he got was a black, sticky mess.

But when he cleaned it with alcohol, it turned... bright purple!

He had invented the world's first artificial dye. He quit school, opened a factory, and got rich.

Even Queen Victoria wore it. And suddenly, purple was for everyone.

So if you own something purple... it started with snail slime, and a failed medicine.
