This man got rich selling ice... in India! Two hundred years ago, with no fridges.

His name was Frederic Tudor. In eighteen-oh-six, he filled a ship with ice and sent it to the Caribbean.

People laughed. A Boston newspaper joked, "We hope this will not prove to be a slippery speculation."

It was. Most of the ice melted, and nobody there knew what to do with the rest.

Tudor lost everything, and ended up in debtors' prison.

But he didn't quit. He packed his ice in sawdust, so it melted much slower.

Then he gave ice away for free. He showed bartenders how to make cold drinks, and soon, everyone wanted them.

In eighteen thirty-three, he tried his craziest trip yet. Sixteen thousand miles, to Calcutta, India. Four months at sea.

People expected a ship full of water. But about a hundred tons of ice made it!

Tudor became known as the Ice King, and made a fortune.

So next time you get ice in your drink, thank the man everyone laughed at.
