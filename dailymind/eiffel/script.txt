The Eiffel Tower was supposed to be torn down... and a radio saved it.

Paris, eighteen-eighty-nine. Gustave Eiffel builds the tallest structure on Earth for the World's Fair.

Parisians hate it. Famous artists sign a protest, calling it useless... and monstrous.

And Eiffel only gets a permit for twenty years. In nineteen-oh-nine... it comes down.

So Eiffel gets clever. He makes the tower useful.

He puts a weather station on top. He drops objects down a cable to study air resistance.

Then he pays for a giant radio antenna... with his own money.

The army loves it. Messages fly across France.

Then World War One begins. The tower catches enemy radio messages... and helps catch a famous spy.

Now nobody dares to tear it down... and today, it's the most visited paid monument on Earth.
